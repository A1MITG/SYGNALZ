One month.

01Experiment one

A month ago, I wrote about getting back to building.

Signal was the first experiment. It started with a simple question:

What do I actually want to read?

02Experiment two

Now comes Vantage 360.

The second experiment. The next rabbit hole. And another opportunity to discover how much I don't know.

This time, the question is different:

What if you could understand a company, its market, its competitors, and its opportunities without spending days digging through information?

That's the idea behind Vantage 360.

03What it delivers

A market intelligence and research engine designed to help people make better decisions.

It takes information about a company, its market, its competitors and its opportunities, and turns it into insight.

  • Information
  • →Insight
  • →Better decisions

Not another dashboard full of data. Not another AI-generated report that sounds impressive but tells you very little.

The ambition is to turn information into insight, and insight into better decisions.

That's the north star.

04The journey

The journey there? Still very much a work in progress.

I'm building with AI, experimenting with different approaches, breaking things, fixing them, and occasionally wondering why something that looked so simple on paper has taken three hours to get right.

The fun part? Every problem solved opens up three new questions.

And that's exactly why I'm enjoying this.

Two experiments in a month. A little more discipline. A clearer direction. And plenty left to learn.

05Building in public

I'm building in public.

Expect to see the progress, the mistakes, the occasional breakthrough, and probably a few questionable decisions along the way.

Feedback from friends, users, and fellow builders is always welcome. Especially the kind that makes me rethink something I've been convinced was brilliant.

Because the goal isn't to get everything right the first time.

  • Keep building.
  • Keep learning.
  • Get a little better with every iteration.

Signal was the first. Vantage 360 is the second.

Let's see what comes next.
