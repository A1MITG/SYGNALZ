The Builder’s Manifesto

Hands-on.
Hands-off.
Hands-on again.

01Who I am

The itch to build never went away.

I started as a developer, solving problems in code.

Then I led people: bigger teams, bigger problems, outcomes at scale. Every step took me further from the keyboard and closer to strategy and execution.

That was the job.

But I wasn’t going to spend my weekends writing Excel macros and VBA. 😄

Then came AI. Then came Claude. And suddenly I could build again, without becoming a full-time developer again.

So I’m back.

02What I’m building

Signal is my first experiment.

It started with one question:

What do I actually want to read?

Today, Signal gathers news from many sources and shows it through different lenses. That’s Phase One.

The goal is to turn that information, step by step, into intelligence:

  • What happened
  • →Why it matters
  • →Who it affects
  • →What to watch
  • →What to do

Read, above all, for what it means for India, Indians and Indian businesses.

03Where I am

Laying the foundations. Deliberately unfinished.

Sources. Signals. Themes. Lenses. AI. Code.

Testing ideas, breaking things, fixing them, and learning by building.

  • No grand business plan.
  • No startup pitch.
  • Just a builder, building.
04What’s next

You decide what matters to you. Signal finds the signals.

The next version moves beyond aggregation:

  • News
  • →Context
  • →Intelligence
  • →Action

And Signal won’t be the last experiment.

Build. Learn. Iterate. Repeat.

Let’s see where it goes.
